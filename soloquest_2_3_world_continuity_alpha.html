⚔ Combat encounter

The AI DM creates encounters automatically. You roll your own initiative and attacks. Enemies use local dice and provisional starter profiles. Movement uses abstract distances, not a battle map. Monster profiles are provisional, not verified 2024 stat blocks. No manual enemy entry.

Position & movement

Abstract distance in feet; no terrain, cover, or grid yet. Enemy reactions and spell effects remain incomplete.

Combat effects & concentration

DM adjudicates conditions; the engine enforces selected attack effects. These controls are temporary until automatic spell/monster effects are integrated.

Turn tracker, initiative, AC, hit/miss, critical damage, Sneak Attack and HP. AI encounter triggers use preset starter profiles; 2024 combat is still incomplete: full spell lists, spell preparation, Counterspell, movement grids, complete monster actions, and other reactions need further work.

Spellcasting, Reactions & Damage

Rules-assisted prototype; spell eligibility and preparation are not fully validated. Shield reaction is currently implemented only for Wizard and Sorcerer (and future Artificer support); other reaction spells, Counterspell, and full spell preparation remain unsupported.

Reactions reset at the start of your next turn. Shield is offered when an enemy attack hits.

Damage effects

Resistance choice applies to the player’s next weapon/spell hit against the selected target. Temporary HP absorbs damage before normal HP.

Character Creator

Rule-guided builder: choose a class, species and background. Apply & Save Character and background bonuses to calculate scores and HP. Existing imported stats remain unchanged until you save. Origin feats are assigned by background; subclass options unlock at level 3. Other class and species features are not yet automated. Finalization locks your choices.

2024 Point Buy helper

Set six base scores (8–15) using 27 points. Choose base scores and apply permitted background bonuses. Final scores are calculated automatically.

HP uses fixed average on level-up. No manual maximum HP override. AC is still manual until armor and equipment rules are implemented.

Skills & Expertise

Choose class skill proficiencies and Rogue Expertise. Background and species proficiencies will be expanded later.

Saving Throws

Level up requires confirmation. At feat/ASI levels, use a separate rules decision; the automatic button stops rather than granting arbitrary stats.
